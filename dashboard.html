Performance Evaluation

The strategy generated positive returns during training under both fee scenarios. However, the out-of-sample test generated negative returns under both scenarios. At a 0.2% fee per side, the test return was -52.49% with a maximum drawdown of -57.49%. The strategy has not demonstrated robust out-of-sample performance and is not considered ready for live trading.

The 0.1% and 0.2% fee scenarios are separate historical simulations. Both use an assumed full spread of 0.04% and slippage of 0.05% per side. These are modeled costs, not actual historical execution costs. The forward test uses a 0.2% taker fee and orderbook-based execution simulation.

Historical backtest results are separate from the ongoing forward test. The forward test started on November 11, 2026 and has not completed the originally planned 10-day evaluation period. It must not be interpreted as a completed 10-day test.
